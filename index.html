About Me

Welcome to my portfolio! I’m Aakash Ninan Gikku, an aspiring software developer and tech enthusiast with a deep curiosity for how technology shapes the world around us. Currently pursuing my Integrated Master of Computer Applications (IMCA), I’m continuously exploring new technologies, frameworks, and design principles to build efficient, meaningful digital solutions.

From crafting responsive websites to experimenting with emerging tools and programming languages, I enjoy transforming ideas into working applications. I believe in writing clean, maintainable code and creating user experiences that are simple, accessible, and visually appealing.

My journey so far has been filled with exciting challenges, from classroom projects and collaborative assignments to independent explorations in web development and programming. Each step has strengthened my problem-solving skills, creativity, and ability to adapt to evolving tech trends.

When I’m not coding, you’ll find me unwinding with my other passions. I'm an avid cricket fan, always ready for the next big match, and I love diving into the world of movies. I also have a keen interest in politics and current affairs, staying informed about the world around me.
